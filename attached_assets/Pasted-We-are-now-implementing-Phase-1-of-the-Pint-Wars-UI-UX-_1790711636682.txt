FIRST TASK ONLY: Fix the paid league capacity/product mismatch.

The audit found that a user can select one league size in Create a Pint War and then purchase a different capacity through RevenueCat. The actual purchased RevenueCat product currently determines the league capacity.

This must be made impossible/confusion-free.

- 6 players = £2.99
- 10 players = £3.99
- 14 players = £4.99
- 16 players = £5.99
- Paid duration = 1–30 days
- Price depends ONLY on player capacity
- Do not change any prices
- Do not create new RevenueCat products
- Do not change the existing RevenueCat product IDs
- Do not change the duration rules

Requirements:

1. When the user selects a league size, checkout must correspond ONLY to that selected size.
2. The user must not be able to accidentally purchase a different capacity from the RevenueCat checkout.
3. Before purchase, clearly show:
   - selected player capacity
   - price
   - selected duration
4. After RevenueCat purchase succeeds, verify that the purchased product corresponds to the selected capacity before creating the league.
5. If the purchased product does not match the selected capacity, do NOT create the league. Show a clear user-facing error and allow recovery.
6. Preserve the existing RevenueCat purchase verification/webhook flow.
7. Preserve server-side verification. Do not trust the client alone.
8. Do not change Supabase scoring, duration, account deletion or any unrelated functionality.
9. Do not redesign the entire Create a Pint War screen yet.
10. Do not add new features.
