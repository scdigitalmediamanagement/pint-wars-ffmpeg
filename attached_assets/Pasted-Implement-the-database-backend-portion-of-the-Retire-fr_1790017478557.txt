- Their other leagues are unaffected.
- Existing pint logs remain.
- Existing score events remain.
- Existing points remain.
- Membership changes from `active` to `retired`.
- They cannot create any new score in that league.
- They can still view the league and their historical score.
- `removed` remains a separate host moderation state.
