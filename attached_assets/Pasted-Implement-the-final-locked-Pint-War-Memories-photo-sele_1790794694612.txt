Implement the final locked Pint War Memories photo-selection behaviour.

IMPORTANT:
Do not change the rest of the Pint Wars app.

LOCKED MEMORIES PRODUCT:

When a completed Pint War user taps “Create Memories”, create a roughly 30-second fun highlight experience using photos captured during the ENTIRE Pint War.

The current implementation uses up to 8 photos. Replace that behaviour.

PHOTO COUNT:
- Target approximately 16–20 photos for a typical 30-second Memories video/preview.
- Do NOT hard-code exactly 16 or exactly 20.
- Choose the number dynamically based on available usable photos.
- If fewer than 16 photos exist, use all usable photos.
- If more than 20 exist, intelligently select a varied subset.
- Never duplicate a photo.

SMART RANDOM SELECTION:
Selection must be randomized, but not purely random.

The selector should:
- Cover the entire Pint War where possible.
- Give different players representation.
- Give different pubs representation where possible.
- Avoid consecutive excessive repetition of the same player or pub.
- Avoid duplicate photos.
- Randomize the selected sequence each time Memories is generated.

END-OF-NIGHT EMPHASIS:
This is important.

The Memories experience should deliberately capture the final part of the night.

For each completed Pint War:
- Identify the final portion of the war using the available authoritative event timestamps.
- Give late-night/end-of-war photos a higher selection weight than ordinary earlier photos.
- Still retain photos from earlier in the Pint War.
- Do not make the final video chronological.
- Do not simply use the last 16–20 photos.
- The final result should feel spontaneous while giving the ending of the night extra representation.

For a multi-day war:
- Include photos from across the duration.
- Give extra weight to the final evening/night.

For a short weekend war:
- Give particularly strong representation to the final night.

VIDEO/PREVIEW:
Keep the target around 30 seconds.

The experience should feel:
- Fun
- Fast
- Social
- Slightly chaotic
- Like reliving a great night out with mates

Use:
- Fast photo changes
- Simple transitions
- Occasional stats/event cards
- Final winner/results reveal

Do not turn it into a chronological report.

MUSIC:
Do not add a third-party music service yet.
Do not use copyrighted commercial music.
Keep the architecture ready for a suitable licensed/royalty-free track to be added when actual video export is implemented.

SECURITY:
Continue using the existing member-authorized private photo access.
Do not make Storage public.
Do not expose Storage paths or unrestricted URLs.
Only members of the completed Pint War can access the photos.

IMPORTANT:
Do not implement actual MP4 export yet unless the existing architecture already supports it safely.
The current target remains the correct Memories experience/data selection layer.
