Implement Phase 1 Task 5 only: improve the Log a Pint experience.

The underlying pint logging, scoring and Live Activity functionality already works and has been tested on real devices.

Do NOT change the backend scoring rules or pint-log architecture.

LOCKED SCORING:
- 1 logged pint = +1 point
- Qualifying pub review = +1 bonus
- No new-pub bonus
- Review bonus only once per player per pub

Improve the user experience from tapping “Log a Pint” through successful submission.

DESIRED FLOW:

1. User taps Log a Pint.
2. Clearly explain that they need a fresh proof photo.
3. Open the camera.
4. Capture the photo.
5. Clearly show the captured photo before submission.
6. Allow the user to retake/cancel.
7. Identify the pub when location/provider data allows.
8. Clearly show the selected/identified pub.
9. Allow the user to continue without a pub when identification is unavailable.
10. Submit the pint.
11. Show a strong success state:
    “PINT LOGGED”
    “+1 POINT”
12. Return/show the updated score and Live Activity entry.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not require location.
- Do not require pub identification.
- Do not change photo-proof security.
- Keep proof photos private to the relevant Pint War members.
- Do not change the existing Storage bucket.
- Do not change scoring.
- Do not change RevenueCat.
- Do not change league duration.
- Do not change account deletion.

UX improvements:
- Make the camera/capture state clear.
- Make Retake and Cancel obvious.
- Make submission/loading state clear.
- Prevent accidental double submission.
