LOCKED SCORING RULES:

1. Every qualifying logged pint = +1 point.

5. A qualifying pub review = +3 REVIEW bonus.

9. Reporting a review awards 0 points.
10. Existing historical pint logs keep their existing +1 base scoring.
11. Do NOT retroactively award NEW PUB or REVIEW bonuses to historical data.
