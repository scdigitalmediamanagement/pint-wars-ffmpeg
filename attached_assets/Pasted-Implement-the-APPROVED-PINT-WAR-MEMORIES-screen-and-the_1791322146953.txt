CORE RESULT

4. Play the generated MP4 inside the Memories screen.
5. Save the generated MP4 to the device/photo library using the appropriate iOS permission flow.
6. Share the generated MP4 using the native iOS share sheet.
7. Show proper generating, success and failure/retry states.
