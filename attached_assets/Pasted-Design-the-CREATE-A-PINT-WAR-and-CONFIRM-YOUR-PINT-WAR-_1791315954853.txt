CREATE A PINT WAR

Include:

HEADER

- “Create a Pint War”

INTRO
- Short, strong copy explaining that the host creates the competition and invites friends.

LEAGUE NAME
- Premium input field
- Example: “Devon Crew”

LEAGUE SIZE
Present the four paid capacities clearly:
- 6 players — £2.99
- 10 players — £3.99
- 14 players — £4.99
- 16 players — £5.99

Also show the free trial option:
- 4 players
- 10 days
- Free

Make the selected size visually obvious with the gold accent.

DURATION
- 1–30 days
- Make the duration selection feel polished and simple
- Clearly state that price depends on player capacity, not duration

PRIMARY CTA
