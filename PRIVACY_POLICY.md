# Pint Wars Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 26, 2026**

Pint Wars (“Pint Wars”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a social competition app. This Privacy Policy explains what information Pint Wars handles, why it is handled, the services involved, and how users can request deletion.

## Information we handle

### Account information
Pint Wars may handle your email address, Supabase user ID, and display name. Authentication is provided through Supabase Auth.

### League and app activity
Pint Wars may handle Pint War names, invite codes and status, league memberships and roles, pint logs, timestamps, score history, and in-app notifications. This information is used to operate leagues, maintain leaderboards and provide app features.

### Pub reviews
Pint Wars may handle venue details, ratings, optional review text, timestamps, and reports associated with pub reviews.

### Pint-proof photographs
When you log a pint, Pint Wars may store a proof photograph in a private Supabase Storage bucket. Associated information may include the private storage path and a SHA-256 hash used for proof and duplicate protection.

### Location information
Location is optional. When nearby-pub features are used, foreground latitude and longitude may be sent to our server so nearby venues can be identified. Pint Wars does not request background location.

### Purchases
Paid Pint Wars leagues are purchased through Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchases. RevenueCat is used to process and verify purchase information. Associated information may include the RevenueCat customer/account identifier linked to the Supabase user ID, store/provider, transaction ID, product ID, league capacity, price, and purchase, verification and consumption timestamps.

## How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts; provide profiles and leagues; record pint activity and scores; provide leaderboards and league history; provide pub discovery, maps, reviews and ratings; process and verify purchases; provide notifications; respond to support requests; prevent duplicate or invalid pint-proof submissions; and maintain security and operate the service.

## Services and third parties

Pint Wars uses Supabase for authentication, database services and private storage; RevenueCat for purchase/customer information and purchase verification; Google Places for nearby-pub searches; Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android for mapping functionality; and a support email service.

When nearby-pub features are used, coordinates may be sent to Google Places, which may return venue identifiers, names, addresses and locations.

## Visibility and access

League participation information and display names may be visible to members of the relevant Pint War. Pub reviews may be visible to signed-in Pint Wars users. Pint-proof photographs are stored privately.

Pint Wars uses HTTPS, authenticated requests, database access controls, Supabase Row Level Security and private storage controls to restrict access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Data retention and deletion

Pint Wars does not currently define one fixed automatic expiry period for all account, league, score, review or purchase records.

Users can request account deletion through Pint Wars account-deletion functionality. Deletion processing may remove pint-proof files, personal visit/location information, authored reviews and reports, and active invitations, and may clear personal profile information. Limited historical league, score or purchase information may remain where necessary to preserve completed league history or for legitimate business, legal or security purposes; historical player information may be de-identified as “Deleted player”.

An account hosting an active Pint War may need to finish or end that league before deletion can be completed.

RevenueCat customer deletion, where applicable, is requested through the provider's process and may complete asynchronously. Third-party providers may retain information where required by law or legitimate business obligations.

## Children

Pint Wars does not have a child-specific account flow and does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app, services, data practices or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
