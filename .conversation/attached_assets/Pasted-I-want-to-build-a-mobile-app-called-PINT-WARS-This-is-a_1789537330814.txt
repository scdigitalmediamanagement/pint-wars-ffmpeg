I want to build a mobile app called PINT WARS.

This is a new project. Do NOT start by building the entire application. First, analyse the requirements below, propose the technical architecture and project structure, and then wait for my approval before implementing anything.

PINT WARS is a social pub competition app.

CORE CONCEPT:
Users create or join private Pint Wars with their friends. Players record pints by taking a photo of themselves holding the fresh pint. Each logged pint = 1 Pint War point.

The app is intended for iOS and Android.

V1 COMMERCIAL MODEL:
- Free users can create ONE free active league.
- Free league: maximum 8 players and 30 days.
- Paid leagues are paid for ONLY by the host.
- League sizes:
  8 players = £2.99 for 30 days
  12 players = £3.99 for 30 days
  16 players = £4.99 for 30 days
  20 players = £5.99 for 30 days
- Other players join the host's league for free.
- Extending an existing league for another 30 days costs the host £2.99.
- Once a league has started, the host can add an individual additional player for £0.99, subject to the 20-player maximum.
- A newly added player starts at 0 pints/points.
- Players can retire from a league for free.
- The host can remove players.
- Historical pints, photos and results must remain after a player retires.
- Maximum league size is 20 players.

CORE PRODUCT:
1. Pint Wars
2. Pub Passport
3. Pub Map / Discovery

PINT WAR:
- Create league
- Choose league name
- Choose 8, 12, 16 or 20 players
- Host pays
- Invite friends by share link, invite code and/or QR code
- League lasts 30 days
- Live leaderboard
- 1 logged pint = 1 point
- Host controls league management
- Add player after start for £0.99
- Retire/remove players
- Extend league for £2.99
- League ends automatically after 30 days
- Show final results and winner
- Allow results to be shared

PINT LOGGING:
